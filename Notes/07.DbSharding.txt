                                                DB Sharding
                                                -----------

- The process of storing a large database across multiple machines. 
- A single machine, or DB server, can store and process only a limited amount of data. 
  Hence we split data into smaller chunks, called shards, and store them across several DB servers. 
- All these DB servers usually have the same underlying technologies, and they work together to 
  store and process large volumes of data.

Why is database sharding important?
- As users grow, database becomes a bottleneck if too many users attempt to read/write information 
  simultaneously. 
- DB sharding enables parallel processing of smaller datasets across shards.

Alternatives to DB sharding
- Vertical scaling, replication

Methods of sharding
- Range based, hash based - No need to know in depth
